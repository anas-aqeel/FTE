# Epic Brief: AcademiQ

## Summary

This Epic delivers a conversational AI-powered personal assistant that aggregates information from Gmail, Google Classroom, and WhatsApp into a unified, queryable knowledge base. Students currently struggle with scattered information across multiple platforms, leading to missed deadlines, overlooked assignments, and the embarrassment of being uninformed about critical academic commitments. The assistant provides natural language access to schedules, deadlines, announcements, and project details through a web-based chat interface, eliminating the need to manually check multiple apps and search through noisy group chats. Phase 1 focuses on core data ingestion and conversational query capabilities, enabling students to ask "What's my schedule today?" or "What are my upcoming deadlines?" and receive comprehensive, accurate answers instantly.

## Context & Problem

### Who's Affected

Students managing academic commitments across multiple communication platforms (Gmail, Google Classroom, WhatsApp). These students receive critical information—assignment deadlines, project details, class announcements, meeting schedules—scattered across different channels, with important updates often buried in high-volume group chats.

### Current Pain Points

**Information Fragmentation**
- Critical academic information is scattered across Gmail (calendar invites, professor emails), Google Classroom (assignments, announcements), and WhatsApp (group discussions, informal updates)
- Students must manually check each platform individually to build a complete picture of their commitments
- No unified view of daily schedule, upcoming deadlines, or pending tasks

**Missed Commitments & Deadlines**
- Students often don't know assignments exist until it's too late
- Important announcements get lost in noisy WhatsApp group chats with hundreds of messages
- Deadlines are missed because information wasn't seen or was forgotten
- Students discover projects and requirements only when asking classmates, leading to last-minute scrambling

**Time Waste & Inefficiency**
- Significant time spent searching through emails, messages, and classroom posts to find specific information
- Repeatedly asking classmates "Is there any update?" or "What's the deadline for X?"
- Manual effort to track and remember commitments across platforms

**Social & Emotional Impact**
- Embarrassment from being uninformed about assignments and deadlines
- Having to beg classmates to add them to project groups
- Feeling behind and unprepared compared to peers who stay informed
- Stress from uncertainty about what's due and when

### The Gap

Students need a single, intelligent interface that:
- Aggregates all academic information from multiple sources automatically
- Surfaces important deadlines and announcements proactively
- Answers natural language queries about schedules, deadlines, and project details
- Filters signal from noise in high-volume group chats
- Provides a reliable, always-up-to-date view of commitments

Currently, no such solution exists. Students are forced to maintain manual tracking systems (notes, calendars) that quickly become outdated, or rely on memory and peer communication, both of which are unreliable and lead to the problems described above.

### Success Metrics for Phase 1

- Student can ask one question ("What's my schedule today?") and receive complete daily overview without checking multiple apps
- Zero missed deadlines or assignments due to lack of awareness
- Significant time savings (30+ minutes daily) from eliminated manual searching and app-switching
- Reduced dependency on classmates for basic information about assignments and deadlines
