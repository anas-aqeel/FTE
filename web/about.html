About AcademiQ

Built by students, for students who are tired of missing deadlines.

auto_stories The Story

Picture this: It's 11:58 PM. You're about to submit your assignment when you realize... you were supposed to submit it at 11:00 PM, not midnight.

The deadline was buried in a WhatsApp group chat with 500+ unread messages. Your professor's email is lost in an inbox with 1,247 unread emails. Google Classroom shows it, but you haven't checked in a week.

Sound familiar? We've all been there. This is the problem AcademiQ solves.

The Problem We're Solving

inbox

Information Overload

Critical information scattered across Gmail, WhatsApp, Google Classroom, and Discord.

schedule

Time Wasted

Hours spent searching through messages and emails trying to find specific deadlines.

psychology_alt

Mental Overload

The stress of manually tracking everything across multiple platforms.

lightbulb Our Solution

AcademiQ is your AI-powered personal assistant that automatically aggregates all your academic information into one place.

Instead of switching between 5 different apps, just ask: "What's due this week?" or "Summarize today's group chat."

Powered by Google Gemini AI, AcademiQ understands context, extracts deadlines, scores importance, and surfaces only what matters.

Our Mission

To help every student never miss a deadline again, so they can focus on learning instead of stressing about organization.
