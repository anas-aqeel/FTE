Frequently Asked Questions

How often will you post updates?

We'll post major development milestones here and send email updates to the waitlist every 2-4 weeks. We won't spam you!

Can I provide feedback during development?

Absolutely! We'll share beta access links and feedback forms as we build features. Your input shapes AcademiQ.

When can I start using AcademiQ?

We're targeting early 2026 for beta launch. Waitlist members will be the first to know when beta invites go out.

Will AcademiQ be free?

Beta access is completely free. We may introduce paid plans later, but early beta users will likely get special pricing or lifetime deals.
