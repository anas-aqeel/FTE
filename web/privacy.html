Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 18, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to AcademiQ. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you join our waitlist or use our service.

Information We Collect

Waitlist Information

  • Email Address: We collect your email address when you join our waitlist
  • Timestamp: Date and time of waitlist signup
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information (for security purposes)

Future Service Data (When Launched)

  • Account Information: Name, email, university affiliation
  • Connected Services: Gmail, Google Classroom, and WhatsApp data (with your explicit consent)
  • Usage Data: How you interact with AcademiQ features
  • Academic Data: Assignments, deadlines, and messages you choose to sync

How We Use Your Information

  • To notify you when AcademiQ launches
  • To provide you with beta access and early features
  • To send development updates and product announcements (you can opt-out anytime)
  • To improve our service through aggregated, anonymized analytics
  • To comply with legal obligations

Data Storage & Security

  • Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest
  • Access Control: Only authorized team members can access user data
  • Third-party Services: We use secure, industry-standard providers (e.g., Supabase for database)
  • Data Retention: Waitlist data is kept until launch or until you request deletion

Your Rights (GDPR Compliance)

If you are in the EU/EEA, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate information
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Limit how we use your data
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Right to Object: Object to processing of your personal data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@academiq.com

Third-Party Services

When you use AcademiQ, you'll connect the following services (with OAuth):

  • Google (Gmail & Classroom): We access only the data necessary to show you deadlines and assignments
  • WhatsApp: Messages are processed locally and summarized; we don't share them with third parties

We do NOT sell your data to anyone, ever.

Cookies & Tracking

Our website currently uses minimal tracking:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for website functionality
  • Analytics: We may use privacy-friendly analytics (e.g., Plausible) to understand traffic
  • We do NOT use invasive tracking or advertising cookies

Children's Privacy

AcademiQ is intended for university students (18+). We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe we've collected information from a minor, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy as AcademiQ evolves. We'll notify waitlist members of significant changes via email. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or your data:

  • Email: privacy@academiq.com
  • Contact Form: Available on our website

TL;DR: We collect your email for the waitlist, will ask permission before accessing your Gmail/Classroom/WhatsApp when you use AcademiQ, encrypt everything, never sell your data, and you can delete your account anytime. 🎓
